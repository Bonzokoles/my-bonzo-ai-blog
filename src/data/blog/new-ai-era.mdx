# AI Agent Revolution 2026: The Dawn of Autonomous Fleets

## Introduction
The year 2026 marks a pivotal moment in Artificial Intelligence. We are moving from simple chatbots to complex, autonomous agent fleets capable of executing multi-step business processes without human intervention.

## The Shift to Agentic RAG
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has evolved. It's no longer just about searching vector databases. It's about agents (like 'The Angels' in Jimbo OS) actively deciding *when* to search, *how* to filter data, and *how* to synthesize answers from multiple sources (GraphRAG + Vector).

## Cloudflare Integration
Using Cloudflare Workers AI and Vectorize allows us to run these agents at the edge, reducing latency to milliseconds.

## Conclusion
The future is here, and it is autonomous.
